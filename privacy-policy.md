# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

## Overview

Personal Drive Sync is a personal-use configuration of the open-source rclone application. It is not a public service and is not intended for use by other people.

## Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive data only to support my own file-management and backup activities.

Access is authorized through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization process. The resulting authorization tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration on my computer.

Google Drive files and metadata are processed by rclone to perform the operations I initiate. I do not operate a separate server that collects or stores users' Google Drive data.

## Sharing and disclosure

I do not intentionally share Google Drive data accessed through this setup with third parties. Google processes data as part of providing its own services, subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## Data retention and revocation

Local copies of files and OAuth tokens may remain on my computer according to my own configuration and backup practices. I can revoke the application's Google account access through my Google Account security settings.

## Contact

For questions about this personal project, use the contact information associated with its repository.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
